# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in CivicPatch a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, publishing private information, personal attacks, and sustained disruptive conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's GitHub contact mechanisms. Include relevant context and links, but avoid reposting private information publicly. Maintainers should review reports fairly, confidentially where feasible, and proportionately. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
